How these checks work
GPS spoofing
Flags a reported position that no road vehicle could reach from its last trusted position (implied speed above km/h). The suspect fix is not trusted for later checks.
GPS jamming
Flags no position fix for more than seconds while the ignition is on and the device is powered.
Device tampering
Flags a tracking device reporting its main power disconnected while the ignition is on.
Odometer integrity
Compares odometer distance with GPS distance every minute; flags differences over 1.5 km and 15%, including rollbacks.
After-hours movement
Flags a trip starting from parked outside operating hours (, ).
Data trust score
100 minus a penalty for each data-quality event in the last 24 hours (spoofing 15, jamming 8, tampering 25, odometer 12). After-hours movement doesn't reduce it.
Evidence export
A vehicle's position history as CSV, where each row carries a SHA-256 hash chained to the row before it. The final hash is recorded in the audit log at export time. Any later edit, insertion or deletion can be detected with scripts/verify-evidence.js. This shows the file is unchanged since export; it does not by itself prove the telemetry was accurate.

    How these figures are calculated
    Period and comparison
    Days are local calendar days. Every figure is compared with the period of the same length immediately before it; a period that runs to now is compared up to the same time of day. Changes are not shown when telemetry history does not cover the whole comparison period. Activity is the change in each vehicle's cumulative counters (odometer, fuel or energy, CO₂, engine time) between local midnights, so gaps in telemetry do not distort totals.
    Emissions
    Fuel and energy use multiplied by emission factors:
    Utilisation and idling
    Utilisation is moving time as a share of engine-on time. Idle fuel is idle hours multiplied by each vehicle's idle consumption rate. A vehicle is active on a day when it travels more than 1 km.
    Risk per 100 km
    Safety events weighted by severity, divided by distance, so busy vehicles are not penalised for simply driving more. Weights: Vehicles more than 1.5 times the fleet rate are flagged. Rates and g/km are not shown below 20 km, where a single event would dominate.
    Findings
    Generated by fixed rules from the numbers on this page. They point to where to look; they do not diagnose causes.
