    How these figures are calculated
    Period and comparison
    Days are local calendar days. Every figure is compared with the period of the same length immediately before it; a period that runs to now is compared up to the same time of day. Changes are not shown when telemetry history does not cover the whole comparison period. Activity is the change in each vehicle's cumulative counters (odometer, fuel or energy, CO₂, engine time) between local midnights, so gaps in telemetry do not distort totals.
    Emissions
    Fuel and energy use multiplied by emission factors:
    Utilisation and idling
    Utilisation is moving time as a share of engine-on time. Idle fuel is idle hours multiplied by each vehicle's idle consumption rate. A vehicle is active on a day when it travels more than 1 km.
    Risk per 100 km
    Safety events weighted by severity, divided by distance, so busy vehicles are not penalised for simply driving more. Weights: Vehicles more than 1.5 times the fleet rate are flagged. Rates and g/km are not shown below 20 km, where a single event would dominate.
    Findings
    Generated by fixed rules from the numbers on this page. They point to where to look; they do not diagnose causes.
