Subscription and Billing Terms

Draft — last edited 10 November 2026. Not yet in force.

Pricing basis

Fleet Shield's self-serve platform subscription is priced per vehicle: AUD $17.50 per vehicle per month, as configured in the live product today. Implementation, integration, and enterprise/government pricing is quoted individually — see the Fleet Shield page for the current commercial structure.

Billing interval

The self-serve subscription bills monthly, in advance. Subscription quantity (the number of billed vehicles) updates automatically as vehicles are added to or removed from an account, and is reflected on the next invoice.

Renewals

Self-serve subscriptions renew automatically each billing period unless cancelled. Organisational or enterprise agreements renew on the terms set out in that agreement.

Payment failures

If a payment fails, the account is marked "past due" and access continues for a grace period while payment is retried, consistent with Stripe's standard retry behaviour. If payment cannot be collected, access may be suspended until it's resolved.
